Road to the Platinum

Early estimates put the Platinum at around 200 hours and 4/10 difficulty (PowerPyx). No trophy needs online play. One trophy may be missable — see Phase 1.

Phase 1 — Play the story

Play the campaign through Chapters 1–4 and the final mission. That alone earns the 12 story trophies, including the gold for the final mission.

Do this first: keep a manual save before leaving Chapter 1. A Chapter 1 meditation with Lucia may count toward Positive Mental Attitude, and it is not settled whether it is required. Do not use cheats — they disable saving and trophy progress.

Phase 2 — Side strands & activities

Finish the 8 stranger mission strands and the finales for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone. Then work the activities: 42 Shooting Range golds, drone races at every Top Fun location, 20 race wins, fishing, arcade, bowling and meditation spots.

Phase 3 — Clean-up & collections

250 stunts, 100 vehicles discovered, 100 WAiNK scans, 3 card sets, 50 challenges, 5 bank vaults and the gang operations. Finish with the in-game 100% checklist for A Long Road — the gold for 100% completion.

⚠ Possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot; a Chapter 1 meditation may be required. Every other trophy can be earned in post-story free roam as far as current guides can tell.
